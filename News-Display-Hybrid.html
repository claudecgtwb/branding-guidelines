TWB launches new terminology hub for health translators

Updated: Jul 10, 2026

Translators Without Borders has launched a new searchable terminology hub built specifically for health translators working in crisis and humanitarian settings. The database covers maternal health, epidemic response, and mental health support terminology across more than 30 languages.

The hub was developed with input from over 200 volunteer linguists who flagged recurring terminology gaps while working on health-related projects — from inconsistent translations of clinical terms to a lack of culturally appropriate equivalents for mental health concepts in several under-resourced languages.

"We kept running into the same problem project after project: two linguists on the same team would translate the same clinical term two different ways, and neither wrong," said one contributing reviewer. "Having one shared, vetted reference changes that."

Organizations running health-related projects on the TWB Platform can now link directly to relevant terminology entries from their project instructions. The hub will continue to grow as more terms are submitted and reviewed by the community.

Access to the terminology hub is available to all TWB Platform linguists and partner organizations at no additional cost.
