Description

This handbook is distributed to newly arrived refugees during intake at reception centers. It covers registration, available services, rights, and emergency contacts. Clarity for a non-technical, potentially low-literacy audience is critical.

Project-specific Instructions

Use plain, direct language at a grade 6–7 reading level. Keep official service names in English with a translated gloss in parentheses on first use.
